Ads & purchases
📢

Ads and ad-free

Relic is free to play and paid for by ads. Some videos are optional and give a reward if you watch them to the end.

You have two ways to quiet things down:

⏱️ Watch & Bank
Watch ads to bank ad-free time, up to the limit shown in the app.
✨ Go Ad-Free
Make a one-time purchase to go ad-free for good.

The purchase is handled by Google Play. The price is shown in your currency before you buy, it is not a subscription, and it is tied to your Google account. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. Ad-free changes how often you see ads and how quickly things refill. It never changes the puzzles themselves.
